Extra Bit — Reflexive Pronouns

🔤 What are Reflexive Pronouns?

Reflexive pronouns are used when the subject and the object are the same person, or to give emphasis. They end in -self (singular) or -selves (plural).

Subject PronounReflexive PronounExample sentence
ImyselfI hurt myself while cooking.
You (singular)yourselfDid you enjoy yourself at the party?
HehimselfUmar carried the sack himself.
SheherselfShe made the dress herself.
ItitselfThe cat cleaned itself.
WeourselvesWe cooked the meal ourselves.
You (plural)yourselvesHelp yourselves to the food!
TheythemselvesThey built the house themselves.

💡 Two uses:
1. Reflexive: She cut herself. (subject = object)
2. Emphatic: The principal himself gave the award. (for emphasis)
